UsageNotch for Android 1.9.0 gives you one usage score for all your providers, compares your week with your typical week, lets you write notes on days, and shows what your tokens are worth. Pair it with UsageNotch for Windows 2.9.

**One number for everything**
- **Usage score.** A ring on Overview with one number from 0 to 100: the average of how much of each provider's weekly limit you've used (its main limit when it has no weekly one). It counts up as it appears, says how it moved since last week ("▲ 8 from 38 last week"), and shows each provider's part. Tap it for what it means.

**Your week, in context**
- **Your typical week.** In This week, a ring around each provider fills to your typical week (the median of your earlier weeks) and laps past it when you used more. "Typical week: 3.1×" sits under each provider, and the PC's sentences say how the week compares with both the week before and your usual.
- **Unusual days.** Days well above your usual for that weekday get a small mark on the chart, and the day's card says so ("2× your normal Saturday (60% is usual, from the 8 weeks before)").
- **How sure the numbers are.** When your PC saw less than half of the week, This week says so in plain words. Totals stay complete; only which day and hour things happened is less certain.
- **Notes on days.** Tap a day in History and add a note, such as "Big refactor" or "Exam week". Notes show as dots on the chart and calendar and under This week. They're kept on this phone until you delete them, sync with your PC, and are included when you export your history as CSV.

**Tokens and plans**
- **What your tokens are worth.** This month at API prices against your plan's price ("$412 at API prices in November · your Max 5× plan is $100 a month (4.1× its price)"), tokens for each full limit used, and your model mix over the last 8 weeks.
- **Project budgets.** Tap a project in Tokens to give it a weekly token budget in millions. Its bar shows how much is used, and your PC alerts you at 80% and 100%. Budgets sync with your PC.
- **Plan history.** Each provider's view shows when your plan changed, such as Pro to Max.

**Smoother**
- Switching between 7, 30 and 90 days morphs the bars from one range to the next instead of redrawing them.
- Tabs glide with the same spring as the glass tab bar on Windows. Reduce motion turns both off.
- Polish: the day card's comparisons no longer run into their labels, dollar amounts read "$3,775", and limit names read the same as on Windows.
