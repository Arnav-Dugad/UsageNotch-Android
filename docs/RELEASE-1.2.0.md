UsageNotch for Android 1.2.0 brings the desktop dock to your phone and makes pairing one scan.

**Pair in one scan**
- On Windows, UsageNotch 2.3 shares with your phone itself: open **Settings → Phone** and turn on sharing. On Android, tap **Scan QR code**. No separate Link app, no files to copy.
- No app yet? Scan the code with your phone's camera. The page that opens offers the download, then pairs in one tap. The app always confirms before pairing from a link.
- Already paired through UsageNotch Link? Choose **Switch to built-in** in Windows Settings → Phone. Your phone stays paired.

**Looks and reads like the desktop**
- A dock strip of rings with the real Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and Cursor logos, the desktop's colour ramp, and the "7d" weekly figure. Tap a ring to jump to its card.
- Provider cards laid out like the desktop popup: *Updated just now*, account name (when the dock shows it), Live/Saved/Sign in pill, each limit with a colour-graded bar, amount, live *Resets in* countdown and *Resets today at 3:29 PM*, the Usage inspector chart, and Refresh / Open dashboard.
- Limits use the desktop's own names ("Current session", "All models", "5-hour window"…) instead of internal IDs, plus pay-as-you-go credits and API spend amounts.
- Light, Dark or System theme. Pull down to refresh.

**Widgets in any size**
- Resize from 1×1 to full screen: a single ring, a dock row or column, or the full view with rings and limit bars.
- Rings are drawn exactly like the desktop: outer ring for the session, inner ring for the week, logo in the centre.
- The focus widget shows one provider with a large dual ring and its limits. Previews in the widget picker show the real design.
