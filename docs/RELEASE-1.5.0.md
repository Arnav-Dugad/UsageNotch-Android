UsageNotch for Android 1.5.0 brings the desktop's Stats to your phone, keeps your settings in step with your PC, and makes widgets tick live.

**Your PC and phone, in step**
- **Settings sync both ways.** Budgets, accent colours, provider names, provider order, alert levels and quiet hours chosen on either device appear on the other. A change made while you're away waits on the phone until it's on your PC's Wi-Fi. The newest change wins.
- **Refresh means refresh.** Pull to refresh, the refresh button and a widget's ↻ now ask your PC to read your providers again, instead of resending its last reading. If the PC read them moments ago, the app says so.
- **More than one account.** A second Claude or Codex account on your PC shows as its own provider ("Codex · Work"), with its own budget and colour.

**Stats from the desktop**
- **Chart explorer** in each provider's view: 24 hours or 7 days, the pace as a dashed line with a band of your observed slowest-to-fastest paces up to the reset, resets and events marked, and your budget as a level. Drag to read any moment, with haptic ticks. Pinch with two fingers to zoom.
- **Tokens** in History: what Claude Code and Codex on your PC used today and this week, an API-price equivalent, 30 days of totals, and the top projects and models.
- **Unused capacity**, **limits reached**, **reset reliability**, **work sessions** and **events** in History, for the limit you pick.
- **Provider status**: incidents from the Claude, OpenAI and Cursor status pages appear on Overview and in the provider's view.

**Budgets for every limit**
- Set a budget on any limit, not only the current session: weekly, Opus, Sonnet and more. The ring shows a tick on its inner (weekly) ring too, the widgets' bars show it, and usage alerts include it.

**While your PC is away**
- **Offline estimate**: a dashed stretch of each bar, and "≈ 46% used now · estimate from your pace", shows where usage probably is while the PC is off or the reading is old. It's always labelled as an estimate.

**Widgets and notifications**
- **Live countdowns on widgets.** Larger widgets count down to the next session reset every second ("Claude renews in 1:42:07"), without waking your phone.
- **Before a limit renews**: a reminder 10 minutes before a limit you've used at least half of renews.
- **Good moment to start**: a nudge when starting a session now makes it renew in the middle of your usual busy hours.
- **Alert levels** of your choice (instead of fixed 80% and 95%), and **quiet hours** that silence every alert overnight. Alerts during quiet hours are never replayed later.

**Design**
- One set of design tokens now drives the colours on both apps, so the usage ramp, brand accents and surfaces match the desktop exactly.
