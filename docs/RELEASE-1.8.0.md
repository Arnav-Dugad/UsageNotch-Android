UsageNotch for Android 1.8.0 tells you how your week went in plain English, keeps your history on your phone for good, and shows a likely window for when you'll hit a limit. Pair it with UsageNotch for Windows 2.8.

**Your week, in plain words**
- **This week.** History now opens with a summary your PC writes: a headline ("A busier week than the last"), each provider's week against the one before ("▲ 35%", "▼ 12%" or "steady"), limits you reached, and a few sentences: your busiest day, when you usually work, what your last weekly limit left unused, and your tokens. Both apps say exactly the same thing.
- **Share your week** now carries that headline and up to three of its sentences.

**History that lasts**
- **7, 30 or 90 days.** Pick a range for daily usage, totals, the daily average and the side-by-side chart.
- **All time.** Everything ever recorded for a limit: since when, your total in limits ("61.4 full limits"), active days, limits reached and your best day.
- **Kept on this phone until you delete it.** Every sync saves your daily totals on the phone, so History reaches back further than the PC sends and keeps working while your PC is off, or after you disconnect. Settings → Data shows how many days are saved, exports them as CSV, and deletes them when you ask.

**Clearer estimates**
- **Pace ranges.** "At this pace: limit around 4:10 PM (likely 3:40–4:50 PM)", from the fastest and slowest paces your PC has seen. When the slow end would come after the reset, it says "as early as 3:40 PM".

**Polish**
- **Official logos.** The current Gemini spark in Google's colours and the Codex mark (the OpenAI API keeps OpenAI's). This also fixes the Cursor logo, which Android couldn't draw.
- **What's new** appears once after an update, and any time from Settings → Updates.
