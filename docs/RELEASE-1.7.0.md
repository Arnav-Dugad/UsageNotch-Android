UsageNotch for Android 1.7.0 shows your plan, handles every kind of account, and makes the stats accurate and easy to read. Pair it with UsageNotch for Windows 2.7.

**Every kind of account**
- **Your plan, at a glance.** A quiet badge next to each provider shows the plan it reports: "Max 5×", "Plus", "Pro", "Edu", "Business" and more, on Overview, in the provider's view and on the Focus widget.
- **Monthly credit limits.** Edu, Business and Enterprise seats that report a monthly allowance show it as a limit, "30 of 150 credits", with budgets, alerts and widget rows like any other limit. Rings stay on your session and weekly limits.
- **Credits and free resets as information.** A bought credit balance and a free limit reset ("Full reset (Weekly + 5 hr) · use by Nov 7") appear as clear rows with a note, never as an empty bar.
- **Claude's credit grants** read as "$249.99 of $250".
- **Where this week went.** Claude's own breakdown of your week (Claude Code, chat…) appears in the provider's view.
- **Codex tokens across every device.** With Windows 2.7 the Codex token card and widget can show totals from OpenAI for your whole account, plus your lifetime total, busiest day and longest streak.

**Accurate, readable stats**
- **One full limit is 100%.** More than that is a multiple: a week of 561% now reads "5.6×", five and a half limits' worth. Tiles say "Used in 7 days", "On an average day", "Busiest day" and "Streak" in plain words.
- **Complete totals.** Usage while your PC was off or asleep, and usage right after a limit renews, now counts (on the day the PC next read it). History tells you how much of each day the PC observed.
- **Clearer charts.** Daily bars show a "1 full limit" line and your average. A day's card shows its share of the limit, how much of the day was observed, and how it compares with usual ("1.6× more" or "30% less").
- **Plainer words everywhere.** Reset reliability says how many periods kept their reset time, work sessions say "% of the limit", and the share card and weekly recap use the same wording.
